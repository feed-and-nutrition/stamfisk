# Stamfisk

[![DOI](https://zenodo.org/badge/722204801.svg)](https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.10200496)

A comprehensive nutritional data repository of Atlantic salmon broodstock.

🌐 **[View the website](https://feed-and-nutrition.github.io/stamfisk/)**

## About

Stamfisk presents data and statistical analyses from a two-year feeding experiment with Atlantic salmon (*Salmo salar*) broodstock. The experiment covers two phases:

- **Phase 1** — Nutrient analysis of female broodstock during the saltwater period (January 2020 – June 2021), comparing long-term (LF) and short-term (SF) feeding groups.
- **Phase 2** — Spawning across three artificially adjusted seasons in freshwater, including sampling of both broodstock and offspring.

## Contents

The site provides raw data, general statistics, and visualisations for:

- Vitamin C
- Fat-soluble vitamins (D, E & K)
- Body weight and tissue weights
- Nutritional composition of experimental diets
